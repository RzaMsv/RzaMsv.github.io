About

I work at the intersection of machine learning, structural bioinformatics and experimental validation. My research focuses on designing insulin therapeutics, combining large-scale sequence mining of animal and venom transcriptomes with generative protein design and wet-lab testing.

I build end-to-end computational pipelines, from sequence curation and language-model embeddings to structure prediction, docking and automated high-throughput screening, and I take the best designs into yeast display and in vivo studies.

Research Projects

Evolution of the Insulin Family Across the Animal Kingdom

First author

Curated more than 13,000 insulin-family sequences across animal species and identified over 5,000 previously uncharacterized variants. Discovered 8 alternative insulin scaffolds with enhanced stability and solubility, informing the design of high-potency analogs.

Sequence bioinformaticsPhylogeneticsData curation

Computational Design of Fast-Acting Human Insulin Analogs

First author

Mined venom transcriptomes across 300+ species to find insulin-like sequences and used them to design 4 human insulin analogs aimed at faster receptor dissociation. Funded through an NIH T32 training grant in computational approaches to diabetes research.

TranscriptomicsProtein designMolecular modeling

Engineering Enhanced-Potency Venom-Derived Insulin

Co-author

Designed 15 mutants of a venom-derived insulin. Ten showed improved receptor binding, five came close to human potency, and two reached the human range and advanced to preclinical pig trials.

Structure-guided designReceptor bindingPreclinical translation

AI-Guided Design of Single-Chain Insulin Variants

Co-author

Built an automated pipeline that generates and screens more than 10,000 single-chain insulin variants using ProteinMPNN, AlphaFold, ESM-2 and ZDOCK. The top 500 candidates go on to yeast display validation.

ProteinMPNNAlphaFoldESM-2High-throughput screening

Functional Annotation of the Dark Venom Peptidome

First author

Developing a multimodal deep learning framework, combining protein language model and graph neural network embeddings, trained on a dataset of 1.5 million peptide-receptor entries to annotate venom peptides of unknown function.

PyTorchProtein language modelsGraph neural networks
